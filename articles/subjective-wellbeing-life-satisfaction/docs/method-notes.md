# Method Notes

This scaffold treats subjective well-being as an evaluative and experiential construct rather than as a complete theory of the good life.

A simple conceptual model is:

```text
subjective well-being =
  life satisfaction
+ positive affect
- negative affect
+ social support
+ income security
+ meaning alignment
- stress load
```

## Interpretation principles

- Do not reduce flourishing to life satisfaction alone.
- Distinguish cognitive evaluation from affective experience.
- Interpret self-report data in relation to culture, adaptation, expectations, and comparison standards.
- Use subjective indicators alongside objective and institutional context.
- Disaggregate results where data permits.
- Do not use sample workflows as diagnostic, clinical, employment, or screening tools.
