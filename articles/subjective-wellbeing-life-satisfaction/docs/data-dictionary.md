# Data Dictionary

| Variable | Type | Direction | Description |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `life_satisfaction` | numeric | higher is better | Cognitive evaluation of life as a whole. |
| `positive_affect` | numeric | higher is better | Frequency or salience of pleasant emotional experience. |
| `negative_affect` | numeric | higher is worse | Frequency or salience of unpleasant emotional experience. |
| `social_support` | numeric | higher is better | Perceived relational support, belonging, and available help. |
| `income_security` | numeric | higher is better | Material stability and reduced financial precarity. |
| `meaning_alignment` | numeric | higher is better | Sense that life activities cohere with purpose, values, and meaning. |
| `stress_load` | numeric | higher is worse | Stressor exposure, strain, and cumulative pressure. |
| `swb_index` | numeric | higher is better | Composite subjective well-being score combining life satisfaction, positive affect, and reverse-coded negative affect. |
