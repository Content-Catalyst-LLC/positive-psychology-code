# Method Notes

This companion scaffold treats Hope Theory as a structured model of goal-directed cognition and motivation.

A working hope model is:

```text
hope =
  agency
+ pathways
```

A context-sensitive pathway model is:

```text
net pathway context =
  pathways
+ social support
+ resource access
- obstacle intensity
```

A future-orientation model is:

```text
net future orientation =
  agency
+ pathways
+ goal clarity
+ goal progress
+ meaning
- stress load
- obstacle intensity
```

A responsible hope-support model is:

```text
effective hope support =
  goal clarity support
+ agency support
+ pathways support
+ resource access support
+ obstacle mapping quality
+ structural barrier review
+ responsible language
```

## Recommended research workflow

1. Inspect missingness, distributions, and scale direction.
2. Direction-correct stress load and obstacle intensity.
3. Evaluate reliability separately by item family.
4. Preserve agency and pathways separately before building total hope scores.
5. Measure goals, obstacles, support, and resources alongside hope.
6. Model goal progress longitudinally where possible.
7. Use network models as exploratory hope-dynamics maps, not causal proof.
8. Assess goal revision quality, not only persistence.
9. Interpret results in relation to context, power, culture, hierarchy, disability access, resources, and institutional conditions.
10. Avoid treating hope scores as clinical measures, employment tools, disciplinary tools, or individual assessments.

## Scope and use

These examples use synthetic data for research workflow demonstration, methods development, documentation, and reproducible analysis. They are not designed for clinical diagnosis, treatment planning, therapeutic intervention, workplace screening, employment selection, student ranking, employee evaluation, school disciplinary decisions, benefits eligibility decisions, or individual psychological assessment.
