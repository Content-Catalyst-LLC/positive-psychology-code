# Validation Plan

This plan is intended for professional research use with real, ethically collected Hope Theory data.

## Data validation

- Confirm data provenance and collection context.
- Confirm domain, measurement timing, role, power relationship, and setting.
- Confirm scale direction and units.
- Check missingness by domain, wave, participant, subgroup, item, and construct.
- Evaluate whether missingness reflects mistrust, burden, low access, language barriers, disability barriers, institutional vulnerability, or demoralization.
- Inspect outliers and impossible values.
- Document inclusion, exclusion, and transformation rules.

## Indicator validation

- Estimate reliability for agency, pathways, goal clarity, goal progress, well-being, meaning, stress, obstacles, social support, and resource access separately.
- Inspect correlations among hope components, obstacles, resources, meaning, goal progress, and well-being.
- Test dimensional structure using PCA, factor analysis, or confirmatory modeling where appropriate.
- Avoid collapsing agency and pathways before checking whether their separate structure matters.
- Report sensitivity to weighting and direction-correction choices.

## Model validation

- Test whether agency and pathways jointly predict goal progress.
- Test whether obstacles moderate the association between pathways and goal progress.
- Test whether support and resource access buffer obstacle intensity.
- Test whether goal progress and meaning mediate or explain links between hope and well-being.
- Compare domain-level patterns across education, health, counseling, career, and community contexts.

## Ethical validation

- Avoid interpreting low hope as personal failure without evaluating blocked pathways and structural barriers.
- Avoid using hope measures for high-stakes individual screening, ranking, discipline, hiring, or evaluation.
- Treat goals, obstacles, future orientation, and well-being data as sensitive.
- Document limits clearly in outputs and article-adjacent interpretation.
