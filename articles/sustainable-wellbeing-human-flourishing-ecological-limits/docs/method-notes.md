# Method Notes

The scaffold treats sustainable well-being as a systems outcome rather than a private psychological state.

A simple conceptual model is:

```text
sustainable well-being =
  psychological functioning
+ social cohesion
+ institutional quality
+ ecological integrity
- carbon pressure
- inequality and precarity
```

The composite examples use transparent weights so assumptions can be inspected and changed. In real analysis, researchers should test alternative weights, compare complete-case and imputed results, and avoid treating the composite score as a final measure of human flourishing.
