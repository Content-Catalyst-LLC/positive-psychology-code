# Data Dictionary

| Variable | Type | Direction | Description |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `life_satisfaction` | numeric | higher is better | Subjective evaluation of life as a whole. |
| `meaning` | numeric | higher is better | Perceived purpose, coherence, and life meaning. |
| `health` | numeric | higher is better | General health indicator. |
| `social_trust` | numeric | higher is better | Trust in community, public life, and social cooperation. |
| `institutional_quality` | numeric | higher is better | Governance capacity, legitimacy, and reliability. |
| `ecological_integrity` | numeric | higher is better | Ecological condition, resilience, and life-support capacity. |
| `carbon_pressure` | numeric | higher is worse | Emissions, energy intensity, or carbon burden indicator. |
| `inequality_index` | numeric | higher is worse | Inequality, precarity, or unequal exposure indicator. |
| `income_security` | numeric | higher is better | Material security and income stability. |
| `civic_participation` | numeric | higher is better | Participation in civic, democratic, community, or collective action. |
