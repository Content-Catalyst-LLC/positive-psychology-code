# Data Dictionary

| Variable | Type | Direction | Description |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `academic_score` | numeric | higher is better | Synthetic academic-growth or mastery indicator. |
| `engagement` | numeric | higher is better | Student engagement, participation, and task involvement. |
| `belonging` | numeric | higher is better | Student sense of inclusion, connection, and school belonging. |
| `resilience` | numeric | higher is better | Adaptive coping, self-regulation, and persistence resources. |
| `life_satisfaction` | numeric | higher is better | Subjective evaluation of life or school-life quality. |
| `school_climate` | numeric | higher is better | Safety, fairness, trust, inclusion, and institutional culture. |
| `teacher_support` | numeric | higher is better | Perceived care, availability, feedback, and support from educators. |
| `purpose_learning` | numeric | higher is better | Connection between learning, identity, future aims, and contribution. |
| `stress_load` | numeric | higher is worse | Cumulative student strain, pressure, and stress burden. |
| `exclusion_exposure` | numeric | higher is worse | Exposure to exclusion, bullying, marginalization, or school disconnection. |
| `access_support` | numeric | higher is better | Disability, language, learning, and access-support adequacy. |
| `student_voice` | numeric | higher is better | Student participation in classroom and school-level interpretation. |

## Implementation-quality variables

| Variable | Description |
|---|---|
| `staff_training` | Strength of educator preparation for positive education work. |
| `implementation_fidelity` | Consistency and quality of program implementation. |
| `student_voice_in_design` | Degree to which students help interpret needs and shape implementation. |
| `family_engagement` | Strength of family/community communication and involvement. |
| `equity_review` | Strength of equity, culture, disability, and access review. |
| `privacy_safeguards` | Strength of privacy, aggregation, and student-data protection. |
| `mental_health_referral_pathway` | Strength of connections to appropriate support when needs exceed universal programming. |
| `teacher_workload_support` | Degree to which educator capacity and workload are addressed. |
| `whole_school_alignment` | Degree to which practices are embedded in school culture rather than isolated exercises. |

## Indicator-bank variables

- `academic1` to `academic2`: academic development indicators.
- `engage1` to `engage2`: engagement indicators.
- `belong1` to `belong2`: belonging indicators.
- `resilience1` to `resilience2`: resilience indicators.
- `climate1` to `climate2`: school-climate indicators.
- `support1` to `support2`: teacher-support indicators.
- `purpose1` to `purpose2`: meaning/purpose-in-learning indicators.
- `strain1` to `strain2`: strain indicators, where higher is worse.
- `equity1` to `equity2`: access/equity-support indicators.
