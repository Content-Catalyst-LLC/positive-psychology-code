# Validation Plan

This plan is intended for professional research use with real, ethically collected school data.

## Data validation

- Confirm data provenance and collection context.
- Confirm scale direction and units.
- Check missingness by school, grade band, wave, subgroup, item, and domain.
- Evaluate whether missingness reflects student absence, survey burden, language access, disability access, mistrust, or institutional exclusion.
- Inspect outliers and impossible values.
- Document inclusion, exclusion, and transformation rules.

## Indicator validation

- Estimate reliability for academic, engagement, belonging, resilience, climate, support, purpose, strain, and equity/access item families separately.
- Inspect correlations among school climate, belonging, teacher support, stress, exclusion, academic development, and life satisfaction.
- Test dimensional structure using PCA, factor analysis, or confirmatory modeling where appropriate.
- Avoid collapsing school domains before checking dimensional structure.
- Report sensitivity to weighting and direction-correction choices.

## Implementation validation

- Document staff training, fidelity, student voice, equity review, privacy safeguards, teacher workload support, and referral pathways.
- Distinguish universal well-being programming from targeted counseling or clinical support.
- Evaluate whether program implementation is whole-school or isolated.
- Avoid attributing outcomes to positive education when implementation was weak or under-resourced.

## Ethical validation

- Avoid moralizing low well-being, low belonging, or distress.
- Treat stress, exclusion, low access support, or low belonging as possible institutional signals, not individual failures.
- Do not use synthetic examples as validated tools.
- Document limits clearly in outputs and article-adjacent interpretation.
