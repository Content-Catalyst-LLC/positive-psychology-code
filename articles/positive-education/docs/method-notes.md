# Method Notes

This companion scaffold treats positive education as a school-flourishing systems problem.

A working conceptual model is:

```text
school flourishing =
  academic development
+ engagement
+ belonging
+ resilience
+ life satisfaction
+ school climate
+ teacher support
+ purpose in learning
+ access support
+ student voice
- stress load
- exclusion exposure
```

Implementation quality is represented as:

```text
implementation quality =
  staff training
+ fidelity
+ student voice in design
+ family engagement
+ equity review
+ privacy safeguards
+ referral pathways
+ teacher workload support
+ whole-school alignment
```

## Recommended research workflow

1. Inspect missingness, distributions, and scale direction.
2. Direction-correct stress load and exclusion exposure.
3. Evaluate reliability separately by item family.
4. Preserve domain scores before building school-flourishing composites.
5. Document weights and test sensitivity to alternative weights.
6. Fit longitudinal models when repeated observations are available.
7. Use network models as exploratory school-system maps, not causal proof.
8. Evaluate implementation quality before attributing outcomes to programs.
9. Interpret results alongside student voice, teacher interpretation, family context, and school conditions.
10. Avoid treating composite scores as clinical measures, disciplinary tools, or individual student assessments.

## Scope and use

These examples use synthetic data for research workflow demonstration, methods development, documentation, and reproducible analysis. They are not designed for clinical diagnosis, treatment planning, therapeutic intervention, disciplinary decision-making, student ranking, teacher ranking, school ranking, employment selection, public-benefits eligibility decisions, or individual well-being assessment.
