# Method Notes

This companion scaffold treats critiques of positive psychology as empirically testable questions about explanatory adequacy, measurement validity, construct travel, structural context, and institutional use.

A working conceptual model is:

```text
critique-sensitive flourishing =
  meaning
+ relationships
+ optimism
+ resilience
+ income security
+ institutional trust
+ cultural fit
+ environmental quality
- inequality exposure
- stress load
```

## Recommended research workflow

1. Inspect missingness, distributions, and scale direction.
2. Evaluate reliability for item families.
3. Compare narrow psychological models against structural/cultural models.
4. Test whether structural predictors improve explanatory adequacy.
5. Model resilience and optimism as context-dependent predictors.
6. Use network analysis as exploratory systems mapping, not causal proof.
7. Track construct distortion when applied uptake rises and nuance falls.
8. Document measurement assumptions and cultural-validity limits.
9. Review institutional-use ethics before applying well-being metrics.
10. Avoid using individual scores for clinical, employment, public-benefits, or screening decisions.

## Scope and use

These examples use synthetic data for research workflow demonstration, methods development, documentation, and reproducible analysis. They are not designed for clinical diagnosis, treatment planning, therapeutic intervention, workplace screening, employment selection, public-benefits eligibility decisions, or individual well-being assessment.
