# Validation Plan

This plan is intended for professional research use with real, ethically collected data.

## Data validation

- Confirm data provenance and collection context.
- Confirm scale direction and units.
- Check missingness by group, wave, variable, and indicator family.
- Evaluate whether missingness is plausibly MCAR, MAR, or MNAR.
- Inspect outliers and impossible values.
- Document inclusion, exclusion, and transformation rules.

## Measurement validation

- Estimate internal consistency for item families where appropriate.
- Inspect dimensional structure using PCA or factor analysis.
- Test whether psychological-only models are under-specified.
- Test whether structural, cultural, institutional, and environmental variables improve model adequacy.
- Evaluate whether measures show cultural or group-specific interpretation issues.
- Avoid treating a scale as a complete definition of flourishing.

## Construct-travel validation

- Document applied context.
- Track scientific nuance retained in applied use.
- Assess institutional accountability.
- Assess privacy and data-governance safeguards.
- Evaluate whether a construct is being used to improve conditions or shift responsibility onto individuals.

## Ethical validation

- Avoid deficit framing of groups with lower flourishing scores.
- Treat inequality exposure and stress load as structural signals, not individual failures.
- Do not use synthetic examples as validated tools.
- Document limits clearly in outputs and article-adjacent interpretation.
