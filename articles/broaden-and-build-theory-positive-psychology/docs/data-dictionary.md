# Data Dictionary

| Variable | Type | Direction | Description |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `condition` | categorical | n/a | Positive-emotion practice or comparison condition. |
| `positive_emotion` | numeric | higher is stronger | Positive emotional activation, such as joy, interest, gratitude, serenity, hope, amusement, awe, or love. |
| `negative_emotion` | numeric | higher is stronger | Negative emotional pressure, such as fear, anger, anxiety, sadness, or threat-related narrowing. |
| `cognitive_flexibility` | numeric | higher is better | Flexibility, openness, creativity, or attentional breadth. |
| `exploratory_behavior` | numeric | higher is better | Exploration, learning, play, experimentation, or novelty-seeking. |
| `affiliative_behavior` | numeric | higher is better | Social approach, connection, kindness, cooperation, or relational openness. |
| `social_support` | numeric | higher is better | Perceived social connection and supportive relationships. |
| `resilience_score` | numeric | higher is better | Adaptive reserve, coping capacity, and bounce-back capacity. |
| `stress_arousal` | numeric | higher is worse | Residual physiological or subjective stress activation. |
| `contextual_safety` | numeric | higher is better | Degree of perceived safety, stability, psychological safety, and support. |
| `resource_stock` | numeric | higher is better | Accumulated psychological, social, intellectual, or coping resources. |
| `practice_fit` | numeric | higher is better | Fit between the positive-emotion practice and participant context. |

## Practice-quality variables

| Variable | Description |
|---|---|
| `emotion_fit` | Whether the practice supports authentic, fitting positive emotion rather than forced positivity. |
| `mechanism_clarity` | Clarity about the broadening, building, recovery, or resource mechanism being targeted. |
| `acceptability` | Perceived usefulness and low burden. |
| `contextual_safety` | Degree to which conditions allow broadening safely. |
| `privacy_safeguards` | Data protection for emotion, well-being, and resilience data. |
| `trauma_sensitive_language` | Degree to which language avoids forced positivity or minimization of distress. |
| `implementation_support` | Supports available for safe and meaningful implementation. |
| `measurement_quality` | Strength of measurement design, documentation, and interpretation limits. |
| `overall_practice_quality` | Transparent average-like summary used for teaching; not a validated score. |

## Indicator-bank variables

- `positive1` to `positive2`: positive emotion indicators.
- `negative1` to `negative2`: negative emotion indicators, where higher is worse.
- `flexibility1` to `flexibility2`: cognitive-flexibility indicators.
- `exploration1` to `exploration2`: exploratory-behavior indicators.
- `affiliation1` to `affiliation2`: affiliative-behavior indicators.
- `support1` to `support2`: social-support indicators.
- `resilience1` to `resilience2`: resilience indicators.
- `arousal1` to `arousal2`: stress-arousal indicators, where higher is worse.
- `safety1` to `safety2`: contextual-safety indicators.
- `resource1` to `resource2`: resource-stock indicators.
