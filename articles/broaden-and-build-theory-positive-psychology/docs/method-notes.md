# Method Notes

This companion scaffold treats Broaden-and-Build Theory as a dynamic system linking positive emotion, cognitive broadening, behavior, resource building, recovery, and upward spirals.

A working broadening model is:

```text
broadening index =
  cognitive flexibility
+ exploratory behavior
+ affiliative behavior
+ contextual safety
+ positive emotion
- negative emotion
```

A resource model is:

```text
resource index =
  social support
+ resilience
+ resource stock
+ contextual safety
+ practice fit
```

A net adaptation model is:

```text
net adaptation =
  positive emotion
+ cognitive flexibility
+ exploratory behavior
+ affiliative behavior
+ social support
+ resilience
+ contextual safety
+ resource stock
- negative emotion
- stress arousal
```

A recovery model is:

```text
recovery capacity =
  positive emotion
+ social support
+ contextual safety
+ resilience
- stress arousal
- negative emotion
```

## Recommended research workflow

1. Inspect missingness, distributions, and scale direction.
2. Direction-correct negative emotion and stress arousal.
3. Evaluate reliability separately by item family.
4. Preserve positive emotion, broadening, behavior, resource, and recovery domains before building composite indices.
5. Document weights and test sensitivity to alternative weights.
6. Fit repeated-measures models when longitudinal observations are available.
7. Use network models as exploratory mechanism maps, not causal proof.
8. Evaluate contextual safety and practice fit before recommending positive-emotion practices.
9. Interpret results alongside stress load, culture, trauma exposure, social support, institutional conditions, and qualitative feedback.
10. Avoid treating positive-emotion or resilience scores as clinical measures, employment tools, screening tools, or individual assessments.

## Scope and use

These examples use synthetic data for research workflow demonstration, methods development, documentation, and reproducible analysis. They are not designed for clinical diagnosis, treatment planning, therapeutic intervention, crisis support, workplace screening, employment selection, public-benefits eligibility decisions, school discipline, employee evaluation, or individual well-being assessment.
