# Validation Plan

This plan is intended for professional research use with real, ethically collected data.

## Data validation

- Confirm data provenance and collection context.
- Confirm positive-emotion practice condition, delivery mode, dosage, duration, and comparison condition.
- Confirm scale direction and units.
- Check missingness by condition, wave, participant, item, context, and domain.
- Evaluate whether missingness reflects nonadherence, burden, distress, low acceptability, privacy concern, contextual unsafety, or context mismatch.
- Inspect outliers and impossible values.
- Document inclusion, exclusion, and transformation rules.

## Indicator validation

- Estimate reliability for positive emotion, negative emotion, cognitive flexibility, exploration, affiliation, social support, resilience, stress arousal, contextual safety, and resource-stock item families separately.
- Inspect correlations among emotion, broadening, behavior, resources, stress arousal, contextual safety, and resilience.
- Test dimensional structure using PCA, factor analysis, or confirmatory modeling where appropriate.
- Avoid collapsing broadening and resource indicators before checking dimensional structure.
- Report sensitivity to weighting and direction-correction choices.

## Theory validation

- Test immediate broadening separately from longer-term resource building.
- Distinguish positive-emotion effects from contextual safety effects.
- Model reciprocal pathways where resources predict later positive emotion.
- Test moderation by stress load, contextual safety, baseline distress, social support, and intervention fit.
- Avoid universal claims where participant context and setting are heterogeneous.

## Ethical validation

- Avoid forced positivity.
- Treat fear, anger, grief, trauma, discrimination, poverty, burnout, or high stress as context signals, not personal failures.
- Do not use synthetic examples as validated tools.
- Document limits clearly in outputs and article-adjacent interpretation.
