# Method Notes

This companion scaffold treats the measurement of flourishing as a multidimensional psychometric and systems-modeling problem.

A working conceptual model is:

```text
integrated flourishing =
  hedonic well-being
+ eudaimonic functioning
+ relational support
+ accomplishment / effective agency
+ health capacity
+ contextual support
- cumulative strain
```

Measurement quality is represented as:

```text
measurement quality =
  reliability evidence
+ validity evidence
+ cultural comparability
+ temporal sensitivity
+ use validity
```

## Recommended research workflow

1. Inspect missingness, distributions, and scale direction.
2. Direction-correct negative affect and stress load.
3. Evaluate reliability separately by item family.
4. Preserve domain scores before building integrated composites.
5. Document weights and test sensitivity to alternative weights.
6. Fit longitudinal models when repeated observations are available.
7. Use network models as exploratory systems maps, not causal proof.
8. Evaluate cultural comparability and measurement invariance where possible.
9. Treat use context as part of validity.
10. Avoid treating composite scores as clinical measures, employment tools, or individual assessments.

## Scope and use

These examples use synthetic data for research workflow demonstration, methods development, documentation, and reproducible analysis. They are not designed for clinical diagnosis, treatment planning, therapeutic intervention, workplace screening, employment selection, public-benefits eligibility decisions, or individual well-being assessment.
