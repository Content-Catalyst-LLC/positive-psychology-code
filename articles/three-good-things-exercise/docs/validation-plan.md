# Validation Plan

This plan is intended for professional research use with real, ethically collected data.

## Data validation

- Confirm data provenance and collection context.
- Confirm scale direction and units.
- Check missingness by condition, day, participant, item, and domain.
- Evaluate whether missingness reflects nonadherence, burden, distress, low acceptability, privacy concerns, or context mismatch.
- Inspect outliers and impossible values.
- Document inclusion, exclusion, and transformation rules.

## Indicator validation

- Estimate reliability for salience, gratitude, support, reflection depth, well-being, distress, and fit item families separately.
- Inspect correlations among life satisfaction, depressive symptoms, gratitude, positive event salience, perceived support, reflection depth, stress load, acceptability, and context fit.
- Test dimensional structure using PCA, factor analysis, or confirmatory modeling where appropriate.
- Avoid collapsing mechanism indicators before checking dimensional structure.
- Report sensitivity to weighting and direction-correction choices.

## Intervention validation

- Track adherence, reflection quality, and acceptability.
- Distinguish standard prompts from adapted prompts such as “three things that helped.”
- Test moderation by stress load, baseline distress, perceived support, and context fit.
- Avoid universal claims where participant fit is heterogeneous.

## Ethical validation

- Avoid forced positivity.
- Treat distress, grief, burnout, trauma, or high stress as context signals, not personal failures.
- Do not use synthetic examples as validated tools.
- Document limits clearly in outputs and article-adjacent interpretation.
