# Validation Plan

This plan is intended for professional research use with real, ethically collected SDT data.

## Data validation

- Confirm data provenance and collection context.
- Confirm domain, measurement timing, role, power relationship, and setting.
- Confirm scale direction and units.
- Check missingness by domain, wave, participant, subgroup, item, and construct.
- Evaluate whether missingness reflects mistrust, burden, power pressure, low access, language barriers, disability barriers, or institutional vulnerability.
- Inspect outliers and impossible values.
- Document inclusion, exclusion, and transformation rules.

## Indicator validation

- Estimate reliability for autonomy, competence, relatedness, need frustration, autonomous motivation, controlled motivation, internalization, well-being, and vitality item families separately.
- Inspect correlations among need support, need frustration, autonomous motivation, controlled motivation, internalization, stress load, vitality, and well-being.
- Test dimensional structure using PCA, factor analysis, or confirmatory modeling where appropriate.
- Avoid collapsing needs before checking whether the three-need structure matters.
- Report sensitivity to weighting and direction-correction choices.

## Model validation

- Test whether need support predicts autonomous motivation.
- Test whether need frustration and controlling pressure predict controlled motivation and stress load.
- Test whether autonomous motivation and internalization predict well-being and vitality.
- Test whether stress load moderates the association between autonomous motivation and well-being.
- Compare domain-level patterns across education, work, health, and sport.

## Ethical validation

- Avoid interpreting low motivation as personal failure without evaluating need-thwarting contexts.
- Avoid using SDT measures for high-stakes individual screening, ranking, discipline, hiring, or evaluation.
- Treat motivation and well-being data as sensitive.
- Document limits clearly in outputs and article-adjacent interpretation.
