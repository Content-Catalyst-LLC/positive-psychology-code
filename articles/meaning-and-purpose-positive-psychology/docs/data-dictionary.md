# Data Dictionary

| Variable | Type | Direction | Description |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `domain` | categorical | n/a | Applied setting such as education, work, health, community, family, or transition. |
| `meaning_presence` | numeric | higher is better | Felt presence of meaning, significance, and life worth. |
| `meaning_search` | numeric | context-dependent | Active search for meaning; may indicate growth, transition, crisis, or incompletion. |
| `purpose_score` | numeric | higher is better | Long-term direction, aims, commitments, and future orientation. |
| `coherence_score` | numeric | higher is better | Sense that life is intelligible, narratively integrated, and understandable. |
| `significance_score` | numeric | higher is better | Sense that one's life matters or makes a difference. |
| `belonging_score` | numeric | higher is better | Connection to family, community, vocation, tradition, institution, faith, place, or cause. |
| `value_alignment` | numeric | higher is better | Fit between action, identity, values, and commitments. |
| `institutional_support` | numeric | higher is better | Degree to which the setting enables meaningful participation, dignity, agency, and contribution. |
| `wellbeing_score` | numeric | higher is better | Synthetic well-being or flourishing outcome score. |
| `goal_persistence` | numeric | higher is better | Sustained action toward valued aims. |
| `stress_load` | numeric | higher is worse | Contextual strain, overload, or burden. |
| `alienation_score` | numeric | higher is worse | Disconnection, meaninglessness, role conflict, or institutional alienation. |
| `identity_integration` | numeric | higher is better | Ability to integrate roles, values, past experience, and future direction. |
| `context_quality` | numeric | higher is better | Overall ecological support for meaningful life and purposeful action. |

## Institutional-context audit variables

| Variable | Description |
|---|---|
| `coherence_support` | Degree to which the setting helps people understand role, story, learning, or life structure. |
| `purpose_support` | Degree to which the setting supports meaningful direction without coercive purpose language. |
| `significance_support` | Degree to which people experience mattering, recognition, contribution, and value. |
| `belonging_support` | Degree to which people experience trustworthy connection and membership. |
| `value_alignment_support` | Degree to which action, role, and values can be aligned. |
| `agency_support` | Degree to which people retain voice, autonomy, and self-endorsed participation. |
| `privacy_safeguards` | Protection for values, identity, meaning, belonging, and purpose data. |
| `cultural_adaptation` | Attention to cultural variation in meaning, purpose, family, faith, community, and vocation. |
| `anti_exploitation_review` | Review of whether meaning/purpose language is being used to extract compliance or labor. |
| `measurement_quality` | Quality of measurement design, documentation, interpretation limits, and validation. |
| `overall_context_quality` | Transparent average-like summary used for teaching; not a validated score. |

## Indicator-bank variables

- `presence1` to `presence2`: meaning presence indicators.
- `search1` to `search2`: meaning search indicators.
- `purpose1` to `purpose2`: purpose indicators.
- `coherence1` to `coherence2`: coherence indicators.
- `significance1` to `significance2`: significance or mattering indicators.
- `belonging1` to `belonging2`: belonging indicators.
- `values1` to `values2`: value alignment indicators.
- `institution1` to `institution2`: institutional support indicators.
- `wellbeing1` to `wellbeing2`: well-being indicators.
- `persistence1` to `persistence2`: goal persistence indicators.
- `stress1` to `stress2`: stress indicators, where higher is worse.
- `alienation1` to `alienation2`: alienation indicators, where higher is worse.
