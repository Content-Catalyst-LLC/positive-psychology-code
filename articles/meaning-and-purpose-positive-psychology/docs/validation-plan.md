# Validation Plan

This plan is intended for professional research use with real, ethically collected meaning and purpose data.

## Data validation

- Confirm data provenance and collection context.
- Confirm domain, measurement timing, role, power relationship, and setting.
- Confirm scale direction and units.
- Check missingness by domain, wave, participant, subgroup, item, and construct.
- Evaluate whether missingness reflects mistrust, burden, low access, language barriers, disability barriers, institutional vulnerability, religious/cultural sensitivity, or role conflict.
- Inspect outliers and impossible values.
- Document inclusion, exclusion, and transformation rules.

## Indicator validation

- Estimate reliability for presence, search, purpose, coherence, significance, belonging, value alignment, institutional support, well-being, persistence, stress, and alienation separately.
- Inspect correlations among presence, search, purpose, coherence, significance, belonging, value alignment, institutional support, stress, alienation, goal persistence, and well-being.
- Test dimensional structure using PCA, factor analysis, or confirmatory modeling where appropriate.
- Avoid collapsing presence and search into a single meaning score.
- Report sensitivity to weighting and direction-correction choices.

## Model validation

- Test whether meaning presence, purpose, coherence, belonging, and value alignment predict well-being.
- Test whether purpose and institutional support predict goal persistence.
- Test whether stress and alienation predict search for meaning.
- Test whether institutional support buffers alienation.
- Test whether context-adjusted meaning predicts well-being better than individual-only meaning.
- Compare domain-level patterns across education, work, health, community, family, and transition contexts.

## Ethical validation

- Avoid interpreting low meaning as personal failure without evaluating context, alienation, blocked opportunity, exploitation, and role conditions.
- Avoid using meaning/purpose measures for high-stakes individual screening, ranking, discipline, hiring, benefits, or evaluation.
- Treat values, identity, faith/spirituality, belonging, purpose, and life-goal data as sensitive.
- Document limits clearly in outputs and article-adjacent interpretation.
