# Method Notes

This companion scaffold treats gratitude as a relational and interpretive contributor to well-being.

A working appreciative-orientation model is:

```text
appreciative orientation =
  gratitude
+ perceived support
+ reflection depth
+ gratitude expression
+ relationship quality
+ social trust
+ intervention fit
- stress load
```

A net well-being model is:

```text
net well-being =
  life satisfaction
+ gratitude
+ perceived support
+ resilience
+ reflection depth
+ gratitude expression
+ relationship quality
+ social trust
- depressive symptoms
- stress load
```

Practice quality can be represented as:

```text
practice quality =
  reflection design
+ mechanism clarity
+ acceptability
+ context fit
+ privacy safeguards
+ trauma-sensitive language
+ relational safety
+ measurement quality
```

## Recommended research workflow

1. Inspect missingness, distributions, and scale direction.
2. Direction-correct depressive symptoms, distress indicators, and stress load.
3. Evaluate reliability separately by item family.
4. Preserve gratitude, support, resilience, expression, relationship, and trust domains before building composite indices.
5. Document weights and test sensitivity to alternative weights.
6. Fit repeated-measures models when longitudinal observations are available.
7. Use network models as exploratory mechanism maps, not causal proof.
8. Evaluate acceptability, context fit, and relational safety before recommending gratitude expression.
9. Interpret results alongside stress load, culture, relationship context, institutional context, and qualitative feedback.
10. Avoid treating gratitude scores as clinical measures, employment tools, screening tools, or individual assessments.

## Scope and use

These examples use synthetic data for research workflow demonstration, methods development, documentation, and reproducible analysis. They are not designed for clinical diagnosis, treatment planning, therapeutic intervention, crisis support, workplace screening, employment selection, public-benefits eligibility decisions, or individual well-being assessment.
