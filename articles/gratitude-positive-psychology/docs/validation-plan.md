# Validation Plan

This plan is intended for professional research use with real, ethically collected data.

## Data validation

- Confirm data provenance and collection context.
- Confirm gratitude-practice condition, delivery mode, dosage, duration, and comparison condition.
- Confirm scale direction and units.
- Check missingness by condition, wave, participant, item, relationship context, and domain.
- Evaluate whether missingness reflects nonadherence, burden, distress, low acceptability, privacy concern, relational unsafety, or context mismatch.
- Inspect outliers and impossible values.
- Document inclusion, exclusion, and transformation rules.

## Indicator validation

- Estimate reliability for gratitude, appreciative attention, perceived support, resilience, reflection depth, gratitude expression, fit, well-being, distress, and relationship-quality item families separately.
- Inspect correlations among gratitude, support, resilience, reflection, expression, relationship quality, social trust, stress load, depressive symptoms, and life satisfaction.
- Test dimensional structure using PCA, factor analysis, or confirmatory modeling where appropriate.
- Avoid collapsing mechanism indicators before checking dimensional structure.
- Report sensitivity to weighting and direction-correction choices.

## Intervention validation

- Track adherence, reflection quality, acceptability, context fit, and relational safety.
- Distinguish gratitude journaling, gratitude letters, gratitude visits, Three Good Things, and adapted prompts.
- Test moderation by baseline distress, stress load, social support, relationship quality, culture, and intervention fit.
- Avoid universal claims where participant fit and setting are heterogeneous.

## Ethical validation

- Avoid forced gratitude.
- Treat distress, grief, burnout, trauma, discrimination, poverty, or high stress as context signals, not personal failures.
- Do not use synthetic examples as validated tools.
- Document limits clearly in outputs and article-adjacent interpretation.
