## Institutional-context audit variables

| Variable | Description |
|---|---|
| `truth_telling_support` | Whether the setting protects honesty, dissent, and responsible truth-telling. |
| `fairness_support` | Whether rules, recognition, burdens, and opportunities are handled fairly. |
| `humility_support` | Whether the setting rewards learning and accountability rather than status performance. |
| `leadership_accountability` | Whether leadership power is bounded by responsibility, transparency, and correction. |
| `care_support` | Whether kindness, compassion, and relationship repair are supported. |
| `learning_support` | Whether curiosity, creativity, judgment, and love of learning are supported. |
| `autonomy_support` | Whether people can express strengths with agency rather than coercion. |
| `anti_coercion_review` | Review of whether strengths data or virtue language could be used to pressure or rank people. |
| `privacy_safeguards` | Protection for moral self-concept, spirituality, character, and strengths data. |
| `cultural_adaptation` | Attention to cultural, religious, philosophical, and local moral-language variation. |
| `measurement_quality` | Quality of measurement design, documentation, interpretation limits, and validation. |
| `overall_context_quality` | Transparent average-like summary used for teaching; not a validated score. |
