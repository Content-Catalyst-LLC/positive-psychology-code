# Method Notes

This companion scaffold treats character strengths as multidimensional, developmental, context-sensitive, and morally interpretable capacities.

A working virtue-cluster model is:

```text
wisdom =
  creativity + curiosity + judgment + love of learning + perspective

courage =
  bravery + perseverance + honesty + zest

humanity =
  love + kindness + social intelligence

justice =
  teamwork + fairness + leadership

temperance =
  forgiveness + humility + prudence + self-regulation

transcendence =
  appreciation of beauty and excellence + gratitude + hope + humor + spirituality
```

A working strength-expression model is:

```text
strength_expression =
  signature_strength_use
+ authenticity
+ contextual_support
- institutional_suppression
- strength_overuse_risk
```

A flourishing model is:

```text
flourishing =
  virtue_profile
+ signature_strength_use
+ authenticity
+ contextual_support
- institutional_suppression
- strength_overuse_risk
```

## Recommended research workflow

1. Inspect missingness, distributions, and scale direction.
2. Direction-correct institutional suppression and strength-overuse risk where needed.
3. Evaluate reliability separately by item family and virtue cluster.
4. Preserve the 24-strength profile before reducing it to virtue clusters.
5. Preserve signature-strength use separately from strength endorsement.
6. Measure contextual support, institutional suppression, power, incentives, privacy, and coercion risk.
7. Model flourishing, meaning, relationships, engagement, and wellbeing separately where possible.
8. Use network models as exploratory strength-system maps, not causal proof.
9. Interpret results in relation to culture, religion/spirituality, institutional context, leadership, role, hierarchy, and moral language.
10. Avoid treating character-strength scores as clinical measures, employment tools, moral rankings, school-ranking tools, disciplinary tools, benefits tools, or individual assessments.

## Scope and use

These examples use synthetic data for research workflow demonstration, methods development, documentation, and reproducible analysis. They are not designed for clinical diagnosis, treatment planning, therapeutic intervention, workplace screening, employment selection, student ranking, employee evaluation, disciplinary action, benefits eligibility decisions, moral ranking, or individual psychological assessment.
