# Method Notes

This scaffold treats workplace well-being as an institutional and systems outcome rather than as a private attitude score.

A simple conceptual model is:

```text
workplace flourishing =
  autonomy support
+ competence growth
+ relatedness and trust
+ work meaning
+ psychological safety
+ supervisor support
+ recovery capacity
- role overload
- job insecurity
- burnout risk
```

## Interpretation principles

- Do not reduce workplace flourishing to engagement or morale.
- Model supportive conditions and harmful conditions together.
- Disaggregate results by role, department, contract status, shift, and relevant equity variables when available.
- Treat survey outputs as institutional evidence, not as employee surveillance.
- Pair quantitative results with qualitative listening and worker voice.
- Use models to identify design problems, not to screen or rank individual workers.
