# Validation Plan

This plan is intended for professional research use with real, ethically collected data.

## Data validation

- Confirm data provenance and collection context.
- Confirm scale direction and units.
- Check missingness by group, wave, item, and domain.
- Evaluate whether missingness is plausibly MCAR, MAR, or MNAR.
- Inspect outliers and impossible values.
- Document inclusion, exclusion, and transformation rules.

## Indicator validation

- Estimate reliability for hedonic and eudaimonic item families separately.
- Inspect correlations among life satisfaction, affect, purpose, autonomy, relationships, context, and strain.
- Test dimensional structure using PCA, factor analysis, or confirmatory modeling where appropriate.
- Avoid collapsing hedonic and eudaimonic indicators before checking dimensional structure.
- Report sensitivity to weighting and direction-correction choices.

## Model validation

- Compare complete-case, imputed, and sensitivity models.
- Inspect mixed-model residuals and random-effects assumptions.
- Test whether longitudinal trends are robust to group heterogeneity.
- Bootstrap network centrality and edge stability.
- Avoid causal claims unless the research design supports them.

## Ethical validation

- Avoid moralizing low happiness, low purpose, or distress.
- Treat stress load and low contextual support as possible structural signals, not individual failures.
- Do not use synthetic examples as validated tools.
- Document limits clearly in outputs and article-adjacent interpretation.
