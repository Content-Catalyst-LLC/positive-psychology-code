# Validation Plan

This plan is intended for professional research use with real, ethically collected PERMA or well-being data.

## Data validation

- Confirm data provenance and collection context.
- Confirm whether data are self-report, interview-based, diary-based, experience-sampled, observational, administrative, or mixed-method.
- Confirm setting, role, power relationship, measurement timing, and institutional context.
- Confirm scale direction and units.
- Check missingness by setting, wave, participant, subgroup, item, and construct.
- Evaluate whether missingness reflects burden, distrust, low access, language barriers, disability barriers, institutional vulnerability, relational sensitivity, cultural mismatch, or privacy concerns.
- Inspect outliers and impossible values.
- Document inclusion, exclusion, transformation, and de-identification rules.

## Indicator validation

- Estimate reliability for positive emotion, engagement, relationships, meaning, accomplishment, flourishing, life satisfaction, institutional support, institutional barriers, autonomy support, fairness, psychological safety, access, and strain separately.
- Inspect correlations among PERMA dimensions and context variables.
- Test dimensional structure using PCA, factor analysis, or confirmatory modeling where appropriate.
- Avoid collapsing the PERMA profile into one score too early.
- Report sensitivity to weighting and direction-correction choices.

## Model validation

- Test whether PERMA dimensions predict flourishing and life satisfaction over time.
- Test whether institutional support strengthens the association between PERMA and flourishing.
- Test whether institutional barriers weaken the association between PERMA and flourishing.
- Test whether PERMA balance predicts flourishing above and beyond the mean PERMA index.
- Test whether the most central PERMA component differs by setting.
- Compare setting-level patterns across education, work, health, community, transition, and public systems.

## Ethical validation

- Avoid interpreting low PERMA scores as personal failure without evaluating real barriers, overload, exclusion, low autonomy, poor access, discrimination, or institutional strain.
- Avoid using well-being measures for high-stakes individual screening, ranking, discipline, hiring, benefits, or evaluation.
- Treat emotional, relational, meaning, life satisfaction, and flourishing data as sensitive.
- Document limits clearly in outputs and article-adjacent interpretation.
