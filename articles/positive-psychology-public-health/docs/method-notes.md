# Method Notes

This companion scaffold treats positive psychology and public health as an integrated population well-being and social-determinants modeling problem.

A working conceptual model is:

```text
public-health flourishing =
  life satisfaction
+ health capability
+ social trust
+ income security
+ institutional quality
+ housing stability
+ education access
+ care access
+ environmental quality
+ community resilience
- cumulative stress load
```

## Recommended research workflow

1. Inspect missingness, distributions, and scale direction.
2. Evaluate internal consistency for indicator families.
3. Run dimensional inspection to identify latent structure.
4. Build transparent composite indicators with documented weights.
5. Test sensitivity to alternative weights and stress-load penalties.
6. Fit longitudinal models when repeated observations are available.
7. Use network models as exploratory systems maps, not causal proof.
8. Report distributional patterns and unequal burden where data allow.
9. Interpret results through public-health, social-determinants, health-equity, and positive-psychology theory.
10. Avoid treating composite scores as clinical measures or individual assessments.

## Scope and use

These examples use synthetic data for research workflow demonstration, methods development, documentation, and reproducible analysis. They are not designed for clinical diagnosis, treatment planning, therapeutic intervention, workplace screening, employment selection, public-benefits eligibility decisions, or individual well-being assessment.
