# Method Notes

This companion scaffold treats post-traumatic growth as a trauma-adjacent transformation process, not as a requirement or guaranteed outcome.

A working PTG-domain model is:

```text
ptg_score =
  appreciation_of_life
+ relating_to_others
+ personal_strength
+ new_possibilities
+ existential_or_spiritual_change
```

An integration model is:

```text
integration_index =
  meaning_making
+ restored_agency
+ narrative_integration
+ social_support
+ context_support
```

A reflection balance model is:

```text
reflective_processing_balance =
  deliberate_rumination
- intrusive_rumination
```

A growth-distress balance model is:

```text
growth_distress_balance =
  ptg_score
+ wellbeing_score
+ integration_index
- distress_score
- ongoing_stress
```

A perceived-corroborated alignment model is:

```text
growth_alignment =
  perceived_growth
+ corroborated_growth
- absolute(perceived_growth - corroborated_growth)
```

## Recommended research workflow

1. Inspect missingness, distributions, and scale direction.
2. Direction-correct distress, intrusive rumination, and ongoing stress where needed.
3. Evaluate reliability separately by PTG domain and support/integration family.
4. Preserve PTG domains before building total PTG scores.
5. Model distress and growth separately; do not assume they are opposites.
6. Distinguish intrusive rumination from deliberate rumination.
7. Distinguish perceived growth from corroborated or behaviorally instantiated change.
8. Use longitudinal designs where possible.
9. Use network models as exploratory recovery-ecology maps, not causal proof.
10. Interpret results in relation to trauma context, culture, safety, resources, power, structural barriers, and timing.
11. Avoid treating PTG scores as clinical measures, crisis measures, employment tools, disciplinary tools, benefits tools, or individual assessments.

## Scope and use

These examples use synthetic data for research workflow demonstration, methods development, documentation, and reproducible analysis. They are not designed for clinical diagnosis, treatment planning, therapeutic intervention, crisis triage, workplace screening, employment selection, student ranking, employee evaluation, public-benefits eligibility decisions, legal determinations, insurance decisions, or individual psychological assessment.
