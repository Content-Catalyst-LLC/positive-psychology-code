# Data Dictionary

| Variable | Type | Direction | Description |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `trauma_context` | categorical | n/a | Broad synthetic event context: illness, bereavement, violence, disaster, accident, or chronic stress. |
| `assumptive_disruption` | numeric | higher means deeper disruption | Degree to which prior beliefs about self, world, safety, control, trust, or meaning were disrupted. |
| `intrusive_rumination` | numeric | higher means more burden | Recurrent unwanted trauma-related thoughts. |
| `deliberate_rumination` | numeric | higher may support integration | Intentional reflection on meaning, values, identity, and future. |
| `meaning_making` | numeric | higher is more meaning work | Effort to interpret, narrate, and integrate traumatic disruption. |
| `social_support` | numeric | higher is better | Quality of support, validation, trust, listening, and relational care. |
| `restored_agency` | numeric | higher is better | Rebuilt sense of action, future orientation, self-direction, and participation in recovery. |
| `narrative_integration` | numeric | higher is better | Degree to which the traumatic event can be integrated into a coherent life story. |
| `context_support` | numeric | higher is better | Supportiveness of the surrounding recovery ecology. |
| `ongoing_stress` | numeric | higher is worse | Continuing stress burden, instability, danger, or strain. |
| `ptg_appreciation` | numeric | higher means more reported growth | Appreciation-of-life domain score. |
| `ptg_relationships` | numeric | higher means more reported growth | Relating-to-others domain score. |
| `ptg_strength` | numeric | higher means more reported growth | Personal-strength domain score. |
| `ptg_new_possibilities` | numeric | higher means more reported growth | New-possibilities domain score. |
| `ptg_existential_change` | numeric | higher means more reported growth | Spiritual or existential-change domain score. |
| `ptg_score` | numeric | higher means more reported growth | Summary PTG score across five domains. |
| `distress_score` | numeric | higher is worse | Synthetic distress outcome score. |
| `wellbeing_score` | numeric | higher is better | Synthetic well-being outcome score. |
| `perceived_growth` | numeric | higher means more self-reported growth | Perceived positive change after trauma. |
| `corroborated_growth` | numeric | higher means more supported change | Synthetic indicator for behaviorally/relationally corroborated change. |

## Context-support audit variables

| Variable | Description |
|---|---|
| `safety_stabilization` | Whether the setting prioritizes safety, stabilization, and basic support before meaning-making. |
| `meaning_support` | Whether meaning-making is supported without forcing positive interpretations. |
| `narrative_support` | Support for coherent, consent-based narrative reconstruction. |
| `social_support_quality` | Quality of validation, listening, trust, and relational presence. |
| `agency_restoration` | Support for rebuilding action, choice, and future orientation. |
| `privacy_safeguards` | Protection for sensitive trauma, recovery, and growth data. |
| `trauma_informed_language` | Degree to which language avoids romanticizing trauma, forced positivity, or moral pressure to grow. |
| `structural_barrier_review` | Attention to material, institutional, safety, legal, and social barriers. |
| `measurement_quality` | Quality of scale selection, interpretation limits, and documentation. |
| `overall_context_quality` | Transparent average-like summary used for teaching; not a validated score. |

## Indicator-bank variables

- `appreciation1` to `appreciation2`: appreciation-of-life indicators.
- `relationships1` to `relationships2`: relating-to-others indicators.
- `strength1` to `strength2`: personal-strength indicators.
- `new_possibilities1` to `new_possibilities2`: new-possibilities indicators.
- `existential1` to `existential2`: existential/spiritual-change indicators.
- `meaning1` to `meaning2`: meaning-making indicators.
- `support1` to `support2`: social-support indicators.
- `agency1` to `agency2`: restored-agency indicators.
- `distress1` to `distress2`: distress indicators, where higher is worse.
- `wellbeing1` to `wellbeing2`: well-being indicators.
