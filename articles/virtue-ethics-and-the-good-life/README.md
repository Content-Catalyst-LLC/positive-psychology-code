# Virtue Ethics and the Good Life

Companion research directory for the article **“Virtue Ethics and the Good Life.”**

This directory connects Aristotelian virtue ethics, eudaimonic well-being, character strengths, practical wisdom, institutional support, and empirical modeling of flourishing. It is designed as a serious analytical scaffold rather than a simple code dump.

## Core Research Questions

1. How can virtue-related constructs be modeled without reducing the moral meaning of virtue to a score?
2. Does practical wisdom moderate the relationship between character strengths and flourishing outcomes?
3. How does institutional support condition the development and expression of character?
4. Can network analysis clarify whether virtue domains behave as integrated systems rather than isolated traits?
5. How sensitive are conclusions to alternative weighting schemes and structural assumptions?

## Responsible Interpretation

Character and virtue measures should be interpreted alongside institutional, cultural, and structural conditions. The purpose of this repository is not to individualize suffering or blame people for hardship produced by injustice, precarity, discrimination, institutional failure, or environmental stress.
