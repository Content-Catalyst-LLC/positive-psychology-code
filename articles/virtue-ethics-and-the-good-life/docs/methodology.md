# Methodology Notes

## Conceptual Model

The article treats flourishing as multidimensional: meaning, relationships, accomplishment, positive emotion, character expression, practical wisdom, institutional support, and stress load.

## Analytical Strategy

The companion code includes:

1. Synthetic data generation with latent virtue, practical wisdom, institutional support, and stress-load variables.
2. Panel modeling of flourishing over time.
3. Interaction testing between virtue and practical wisdom.
4. Network analysis of virtue domains and flourishing indicators.
5. Sensitivity analysis across alternative weighting assumptions.
6. Multi-language examples for portability and engineering reuse.

## Interpretation

A statistically significant relationship between a virtue index and flourishing should not be interpreted as proof that virtue alone causes well-being. Models should be read as structured approximations requiring philosophical interpretation, cultural caution, and institutional context.
