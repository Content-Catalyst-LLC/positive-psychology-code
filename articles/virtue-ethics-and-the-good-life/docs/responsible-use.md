# Responsible Use

Virtue ethics should deepen the study of flourishing, not become a tool for moralizing inequality.

Use this repository with the following cautions:

- Do not use character metrics to blame individuals for structural deprivation.
- Do not interpret synthetic demonstration data as empirical evidence.
- Do not treat psychometric proxies as complete measures of virtue.
- Do not compare groups without cultural, institutional, and historical context.
- Do not use well-being dashboards as substitutes for moral interpretation.
