# Ethics and Scope

This repository is designed for professional research scaffolding, reproducible methods demonstration, and educational use.

It should not be used as:

- a clinical diagnostic system;
- a therapeutic intervention;
- a crisis-support system;
- a mental-health screening instrument;
- an employee screening or selection system;
- a student ranking or disciplinary system;
- an employee evaluation system;
- a productivity-surveillance system;
- a benefits eligibility tool;
- an individual psychological assessment tool;
- a tool for extracting more output from people by maximizing engagement;
- a tool for confusing addictive attention capture with flourishing.

## Appropriate uses

- teaching flow theory and positive psychology research methods;
- demonstrating transparent analytic workflows;
- generating reproducible article companion materials;
- prototyping flow, task-design, attentional-ecology, and recovery documentation;
- exploring synthetic examples of longitudinal and network analysis;
- supporting research planning before validated data collection.

## Requirements for applied research

Any use with real people, communities, workplaces, schools, sport systems, healthcare systems, public systems, digital platforms, or sensitive data should include:

- validated and context-appropriate measures;
- ethical review where appropriate;
- informed consent where appropriate;
- privacy and data-minimization safeguards;
- secure storage and access control;
- cultural and accessibility adaptation;
- review of power, hierarchy, role vulnerability, coercion, and consent;
- anti-surveillance review;
- rest, recovery, workload, and fatigue review;
- disability/access review;
- human factors and ergonomics expertise where relevant;
- distributional and fairness review;
- careful communication of uncertainty.
