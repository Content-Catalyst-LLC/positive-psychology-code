# Method Notes

This companion scaffold treats future well-being science as an interdisciplinary measurement and systems-modeling problem.

A working conceptual model is:

```text
future well-being science =
  subjective experience
+ psychological functioning
+ relationships and social trust
+ institutional quality
+ health and functional capability
+ environmental viability
+ material security
+ civic voice
- stress load
- insecurity and unequal exposure
```

## Recommended research workflow

1. Inspect missingness, distributions, and scale direction.
2. Evaluate internal consistency for item families.
3. Run exploratory factor analysis to inspect dimensional structure.
4. Use confirmatory factor analysis when theory and sample size justify it.
5. Test measurement equivalence across groups when comparing groups.
6. Fit longitudinal models when repeated observations are available.
7. Use network models as exploratory systems maps, not causal proof.
8. Report sensitivity to alternative weights and scale transformations.
9. Disaggregate where ethically and statistically appropriate.
10. Interpret findings with domain theory and social context.

## Scope and use

These examples use synthetic data for research workflow demonstration, methods development, documentation, and reproducible analysis. They are not designed for clinical diagnosis, treatment planning, therapeutic intervention, workplace screening, employment selection, or individual well-being assessment.
