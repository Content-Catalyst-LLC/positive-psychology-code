# Method Notes

This companion scaffold treats well-being and sustainable development as an integrated systems-measurement problem.

A working conceptual model is:

```text
sustainable flourishing =
  human capabilities
+ subjective well-being
+ institutional quality
+ ecological stability
+ social trust
+ resilience capacity
+ basic services
- inequality and unequal burden
```

## Recommended research workflow

1. Inspect missingness, distributions, and scale direction.
2. Evaluate internal consistency for indicator families.
3. Run dimensional inspection to identify latent structure.
4. Build transparent composite indicators with documented weights.
5. Test sensitivity to alternative weights and transformations.
6. Fit longitudinal models when repeated observations are available.
7. Use network models as exploratory systems maps, not causal proof.
8. Report distributional patterns and inequality penalties.
9. Interpret results with development theory, sustainability science, and local context.
10. Avoid treating composite scores as final or neutral measures of progress.

## Scope and use

These examples use synthetic data for research workflow demonstration, methods development, documentation, and reproducible analysis. They are not designed for clinical diagnosis, treatment planning, therapeutic intervention, workplace screening, employment selection, or individual well-being assessment.
