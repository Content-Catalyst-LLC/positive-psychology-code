# Data Dictionary

| Variable | Type | Direction | Description |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `domain` | categorical | n/a | Applied setting such as education, work, health, community, transition, or public systems. |
| `perceived_control` | numeric | higher is better | Belief that action can influence outcomes. |
| `uncontrollable_events` | numeric | higher is more exposure | Frequency, severity, or intensity of events experienced as uncontrollable. |
| `stability_score` | numeric | higher is more helplessness risk | Degree to which negative-event causes are interpreted as stable or lasting. |
| `globality_score` | numeric | higher is more helplessness risk | Degree to which negative-event causes are interpreted as global or pervasive. |
| `internality_score` | numeric | higher can mean more self-blame | Degree to which negative events are attributed internally; interpretation requires context. |
| `motivation_score` | numeric | higher is better | Effort, initiation, persistence, and re-engagement after difficulty. |
| `depressive_symptoms` | numeric | higher is worse | Synthetic depression-adjacent burden indicator; not a clinical score. |
| `agency_score` | numeric | higher is better | Perceived ability to act, revise, seek help, or influence future outcomes. |
| `support_score` | numeric | higher is better | Relational, educational, workplace, healthcare, or institutional support. |
| `mastery_experience` | numeric | higher is better | Prior or current experience that action can produce effective outcomes. |
| `recovery_opportunity` | numeric | higher is better | Availability of second attempts, repair pathways, rest, support, and safe re-entry. |
| `feedback_quality` | numeric | higher is better | Specific, useful, non-totalizing feedback that supports learning and agency. |
| `institutional_fairness` | numeric | higher is better | Fairness, transparency, accountability, and non-arbitrariness of systems. |

## Context audit variables

| Variable | Description |
|---|---|
| `control_opportunities` | Whether the setting gives people real opportunities to influence outcomes. |
| `revision_pathways` | Whether people can revise, retry, appeal, repair, or recover from setback. |
| `feedback_specificity` | Whether feedback is clear, specific, non-shaming, and actionable. |
| `institutional_fairness` | Whether rules, evaluation, access, and outcomes are fair and transparent. |
| `psychological_safety` | Whether people can discuss setbacks without humiliation or retaliation. |
| `support_availability` | Whether practical, emotional, instructional, or institutional support exists. |
| `mastery_scaffolding` | Whether the setting provides graduated experiences of effective action. |
| `recovery_opportunities` | Whether recovery, second attempts, rest, and re-entry are possible. |
| `anti_blame_review` | Review of whether helplessness language is being used to blame people for real conditions. |
| `privacy_safeguards` | Protection for adversity, agency, motivation, and mental-health-adjacent data. |
| `clinical_escalation_protocol` | Whether severe hopelessness, depressive symptoms, or self-harm risk has a qualified escalation path. |
| `measurement_quality` | Quality of measurement design, documentation, interpretation limits, and validation. |
| `overall_context_quality` | Transparent average-like summary used for teaching; not a validated score. |
