# Method Notes

This companion scaffold treats learned helplessness as a dynamic pattern shaped by actual uncontrollability, perceived control, attribution, motivation, support, recovery, and institutional context.

A working helplessness-index model is:

```text
helplessness_index =
  attributional_stability
+ attributional_globality
+ attributional_internality
```

A working control-gap model is:

```text
control_gap =
  perceived_control
- uncontrollable_events
```

A working agency-recovery model is:

```text
agency_recovery =
  agency
+ support
+ mastery_experience
+ recovery_opportunity
+ feedback_quality
+ institutional_fairness
- uncontrollable_events
- helplessness_index
```

A working motivation model is:

```text
motivation =
  perceived_control
+ agency
+ support
+ mastery_experience
+ feedback_quality
+ institutional_fairness
- uncontrollable_events
- helplessness_index
```

## Recommended research workflow

1. Inspect missingness, distributions, and scale direction.
2. Direction-correct helplessness, uncontrollability, and depressive-symptom indicators where needed.
3. Evaluate reliability separately for control, uncontrollability, attribution, motivation, symptoms, support, and recovery.
4. Preserve actual exposure separately from perceived control.
5. Preserve attributional stability, globality, and internality before reducing to a helplessness index.
6. Measure mastery, support, recovery opportunities, feedback quality, and institutional fairness.
7. Model motivation, depressive symptoms, agency recovery, and context separately where possible.
8. Use network models as exploratory agency-system maps, not causal proof.
9. Interpret results in relation to family, school, workplace, health, culture, public systems, structural constraints, and institutional fairness.
10. Avoid treating helplessness-related scores as clinical measures, employment tools, school-ranking tools, disciplinary tools, benefits tools, or individual assessments.

## Scope and use

These examples use synthetic data for research workflow demonstration, methods development, documentation, and reproducible analysis. They are not designed for clinical diagnosis, treatment planning, therapeutic intervention, crisis support, workplace screening, employment selection, student ranking, employee evaluation, benefits eligibility decisions, or individual psychological assessment.
