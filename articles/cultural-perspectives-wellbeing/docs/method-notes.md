# Method Notes

This companion scaffold treats cultural well-being as a cross-cultural measurement and systems-modeling problem.

A working conceptual model is:

```text
cultural well-being =
  life satisfaction
+ social support
+ relational harmony
+ institutional trust
+ material security
+ civic voice
+ cultural continuity
+ place attachment
+ culturally mediated values
```

## Recommended research workflow

1. Inspect missingness, distributions, scale direction, and response styles.
2. Evaluate internal consistency for item families within each group.
3. Run exploratory factor analysis to inspect dimensional structure.
4. Use confirmatory factor analysis when theory and sample size justify it.
5. Test measurement invariance before comparing group means.
6. Fit multilevel models when individuals are nested in cultural groups.
7. Use random slopes to test whether predictors operate differently across groups.
8. Use group-specific network models as exploratory systems maps, not causal proof.
9. Pair quantitative modeling with cultural expertise and local interpretation.
10. Avoid treating international or cultural rankings as final accounts of flourishing.

## Scope and use

These examples use synthetic data for research workflow demonstration, methods development, documentation, and reproducible analysis. They are not designed for clinical diagnosis, treatment planning, therapeutic intervention, workplace screening, employment selection, or individual well-being assessment.
