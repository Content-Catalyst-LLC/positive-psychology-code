# Method Notes

This companion scaffold treats positive psychology and sustainability as an integrated sustainable-flourishing modeling problem.

A working conceptual model is:

```text
sustainable flourishing =
  psychological functioning
+ relational support
+ institutional capacity
+ ecological condition
+ health capacity
+ adaptive capacity
- environmental exposure
- insecurity load
- inequality burden
```

## Recommended research workflow

1. Inspect missingness, distributions, and scale direction.
2. Direction-correct environmental exposure, insecurity load, and inequality.
3. Evaluate reliability for indicator families where appropriate.
4. Build transparent composite indicators with documented weights.
5. Test sensitivity to alternative weights and strain penalties.
6. Fit longitudinal models when repeated observations are available.
7. Use network models as exploratory systems maps, not causal proof.
8. Report distributional patterns and unequal burden where data allow.
9. Interpret results through positive psychology, sustainability, public health, governance, equity, and ecological-systems theory.
10. Avoid treating composite scores as clinical measures, employment tools, or individual assessments.

## Scope and use

These examples use synthetic data for research workflow demonstration, methods development, documentation, and reproducible analysis. They are not designed for clinical diagnosis, treatment planning, therapeutic intervention, workplace screening, employment selection, public-benefits eligibility decisions, or individual well-being assessment.
