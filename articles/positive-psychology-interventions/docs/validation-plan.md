# Validation Plan

## Data validation

- Confirm intervention condition, delivery mode, dosage, duration, and comparison condition.
- Confirm scale direction and units.
- Check missingness by condition, intervention type, week, participant, item, and domain.
- Evaluate whether missingness reflects nonadherence, burden, distress, low acceptability, privacy concern, or context mismatch.
- Document inclusion, exclusion, and transformation rules.

## Indicator validation

- Estimate reliability for gratitude, strengths use, hope, meaning, social support, adherence, fit, well-being, and distress item families separately.
- Inspect correlations among intervention mechanisms, adherence, fit, stress load, depressive symptoms, and well-being.
- Test dimensional structure using PCA, factor analysis, or confirmatory modeling where appropriate.
- Report sensitivity to weighting and direction-correction choices.

## Ethical validation

- Avoid forced positivity.
- Treat distress, grief, burnout, trauma, discrimination, poverty, or high stress as context signals, not personal failures.
- Do not use synthetic examples as validated tools.
- Document limits clearly in outputs and article-adjacent interpretation.
