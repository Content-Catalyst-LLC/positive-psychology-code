# Method Notes

This scaffold treats positive psychology interventions as structured changes to attention, interpretation, behavior, meaning, relationship, and practice repetition.

```text
mechanism strength =
  gratitude + strengths use + hope + meaning + social support
```

```text
net well-being =
  well-being score
+ gratitude
+ strengths use
+ hope
+ meaning
+ social support
+ intervention fit
- stress load
- depressive symptoms
```

## Recommended workflow

1. Inspect missingness, distributions, and scale direction.
2. Direction-correct depressive symptoms, distress indicators, and stress load.
3. Evaluate reliability separately by item family.
4. Preserve mechanism scores before building composite indices.
5. Document weights and test sensitivity to alternative weights.
6. Fit repeated-measures models when longitudinal observations are available.
7. Use network models as exploratory mechanism maps, not causal proof.
8. Evaluate acceptability, context fit, and practice quality before recommending use.
9. Interpret results alongside stress load, participant context, institutional context, and qualitative feedback.
10. Avoid treating intervention scores as clinical measures, employment tools, screening tools, or individual assessments.
