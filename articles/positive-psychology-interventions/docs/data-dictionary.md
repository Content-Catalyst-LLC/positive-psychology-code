# Data Dictionary

| Variable | Type | Direction | Description |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `condition` | categorical | n/a | Intervention or comparison condition. |
| `ppi_type` | categorical | n/a | Intervention family: gratitude, strengths, hope, meaning, kindness, savoring, neutral reflection, or related type. |
| `wellbeing_score` | numeric | higher is better | Synthetic well-being or flourishing outcome score. |
| `depressive_symptoms` | numeric | higher is worse | Synthetic distress/symptom-burden outcome used for demonstration only. |
| `gratitude_score` | numeric | higher is better | Gratitude or appreciative-awareness mechanism indicator. |
| `strengths_use` | numeric | higher is better | Degree of intentional strengths identification/use. |
| `hope_score` | numeric | higher is better | Agency and pathway-oriented future expectation. |
| `meaning_score` | numeric | higher is better | Purpose, coherence, and valued direction. |
| `social_support` | numeric | higher is better | Perceived relational support and belonging. |
| `adherence_rate` | numeric | higher is better | Completion/dosage of the intervention protocol. |
| `intervention_fit` | numeric | higher is better | Perceived person-intervention fit. |
| `stress_load` | numeric | higher is worse | Contextual strain and burden. |
| `acceptability` | numeric | higher is better | Participant-perceived usefulness and non-burdensomeness. |
| `context_fit` | numeric | higher is better | Fit between the intervention and the participant’s setting, culture, and circumstances. |

## Indicator-bank variables

- `gratitude1` to `gratitude2`: gratitude indicators.
- `strengths1` to `strengths2`: strengths-use indicators.
- `hope1` to `hope2`: hope indicators.
- `meaning1` to `meaning2`: meaning indicators.
- `support1` to `support2`: perceived social-support indicators.
- `adherence1` to `adherence2`: practice adherence indicators.
- `fit1` to `fit2`: person-intervention fit indicators.
- `wellbeing1` to `wellbeing2`: positive well-being indicators.
- `distress1` to `distress2`: distress indicators, where higher is worse.
