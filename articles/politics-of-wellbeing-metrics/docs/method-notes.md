# Method Notes

This scaffold treats well-being metrics as political and institutional instruments rather than neutral dashboards.

A simple composite model is:

```text
public well-being =
  subjective life evaluation
+ health and capability
+ social and institutional trust
+ economic security
+ environmental quality
+ democratic quality
- inequality and precarity
```

## Governance-sensitive interpretation

Well-being metrics should be interpreted with attention to:

- indicator selection;
- weighting choices;
- distributional effects;
- subgroup disaggregation;
- rights constraints;
- democratic legitimacy;
- privacy and data governance;
- future-oriented sustainability;
- the risk of metric capture.
